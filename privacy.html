本机功能与联网功能

一解围棋的 KataGo 对弈、题库、教学和本地复盘在设备上运行，无需注册账号。会员验证、学习奖励、主动分享、可选的第三方 AI 问答及 iCloud 同步会使用网络。付费会员可选用的云端对弈和云端复盘，会把相应棋局发送到我们的服务器计算（见下文「云端对弈与云端复盘」）。Mac 版不提供 iOS 的录屏教练与拍照识别功能。

我们处理的信息与用途

  • 设备与账户标识：App 生成随机设备标识和随机账户 UUID，用于会员设备管理、学习奖励及服务器记录关联。账户 UUID 可通过你的 iCloud 在设备间同步。这些标识不是广告标识符，但相关记录可与同一设备或账户关联。
  • 购买信息：Apple 签名的交易凭证、商品及交易标识、App 账户令牌用于验证会员并防止重复处理。Apple 不会通过这些凭证向我们提供你的 Apple ID 密码或银行卡信息。支付由 Apple 处理。
  • 学习任务进度：已学课程、做题等任务计数与奖励领取记录会发送到服务器，用于显示任务进度、发放奖励和恢复奖励记录。
  • 设备与诊断信息：会员服务会接收设备型号、系统和 App 版本。iOS 版还会在模型运行期间采集性能数据，包括内存与 CPU 占用、发热、电量、磁盘读写和网络流量计数，用于分析性能与稳定性；网络计数是整机接口计数，不是浏览历史或网络通信内容。Mac 版不启用这项定时性能采样。崩溃与卡顿诊断是另外一类，需要你每次单独同意，见下一节。
  • 主动分享的棋谱：只有你选择生成分享链接时，对应 SGF（包括着法、棋手名称、注释、结果等已有内容）才会上传到我们的服务器。持有链接的人可以查看；你可在“我的分享”中撤回链接。
  • 主动提交的反馈：你通过邮件或客服发送的描述、联系方式及自行附上的日志，用于答复和排查问题。发送前可查看和编辑内容。

崩溃与卡顿诊断（每次单独询问）

崩溃诊断和上面的性能数据不同：每发送一次都要你单独同意一次，所以单独写一节。它不改变上面说的“对弈、题库、教学和本地复盘在设备上运行”：诊断信息里没有任何棋局内容，本机的围棋计算也不会因为它改到云端。

不包含什么。诊断信息不含棋谱、着法、对局或复盘内容，不含你的姓名、邮箱或手机号，不含你和 AI 的对话与学习记忆，也不含广告标识符或任何可以跨 App、跨公司追踪你的标识符。调用栈是系统生成的，说的是我们自己的代码在哪一步出了错，不是你下了什么棋。需要如实说明的一点：上传走的是会员服务同一条通道，所以我们会记下发出它的那个随机设备标识和服务器接收时间 —— 和本政策上面提到的其他服务器记录一样，它可以与同一台设备关联，但它不是广告标识符。

什么时候问你。只在 App 崩溃或长时间无响应之后的下一次启动询问一次，问你要不要把这次的诊断信息发给开发者。你点“发送”才上传；点“不发送”就在本机删除，不会再问第二次，也不会在后台补发。App 不会自动上报崩溃。发或不发都不影响任何功能，不影响会员权益，也不影响对弈、做题和复盘。

发送的内容。这份诊断由 Apple 的系统框架 MetricKit 生成并已由系统脱敏，我们原样转交、不做加工：出错时各线程的调用栈（函数与模块名、地址偏移）、异常类型与终止原因、诊断产生的时间、设备型号、系统版本、App 版本与构建号、设备物理内存大小及对应的内存档位，以及出问题时正在使用的 AI 模型档位（例如 b10 / b28 / b40；取不到时为空）。

用途。只用于定位和修复崩溃与卡顿：看清是哪个版本、哪个机型、哪档模型在出问题，并核对修复之后是不是真的变少了。不用于广告，不用来给你做用户画像，不与第三方共享，也不出售。我们没有接任何第三方崩溃统计 SDK，诊断直接上传到我们自己的服务器（与会员、任务记录同一套），不经过 Firebase、Sentry 这类第三方服务。

保留多久。诊断记录按天存放，保留 180 天后删除。

你的选择。你可以每次都点“不发送”，不会有任何后果。已经发送过的诊断如果想删，把大致时间和设备型号发到下方邮箱，我们核实后删除对应记录。

自备 API 的 AI 问答与 iCloud

会员可配置自己的大模型 API。只有你发起提问时，问题、对话历史及有关的课程、题目、棋盘、棋谱、KataGo 分析或学习记忆才会从设备直接发给你选择的供应商，不经过一解服务器。API Key 保存在本机不可同步的 Keychain 条目中，不上传到一解服务器或 iCloud。供应商可能收费，并按其自身隐私政策处理与保存内容；请自行确认其服务条款。

棋谱、偏好、对话与学习记忆可通过你的 Apple iCloud 在设备间同步。你可在设置中管理同步，并在 App 内清空相应对话或记忆。本机围棋计算不会因为这些可选功能改为云端计算。

云端对弈与云端复盘

云端对弈与云端复盘只对付费会员（年费或永久）开放，而且只有你主动选择时才会使用：在「自定义对弈」里选择「云引擎」后，这盘棋的局面与落子会发送到我们的服务器，由服务器上的 KataGo 计算应手与分析；提交云端复盘后，会上传你选择的那一份棋谱（SGF）。服务器保存完成计算和结算所需的棋局、任务状态、结果及积分流水，云端棋局断线后可以恢复。积分只能由付费会员购买和使用，按服务器记录结算。本机对弈、题库和本地复盘不会因此改为云端计算。

只有你主动选择云引擎或提交云复盘时，相关局面、着法或所选 SGF 才会上传。云复盘的原始棋谱（包括已有的棋手名称、注释和分支）、任务状态及报告保存于你的账户，用于异步处理、重启恢复及跨设备取回。读取已有报告不重新计算、不再次扣积分。报告和棋谱仅向通过该账户鉴权的客户端提供，不会自动生成公开分享链接。旧任务若未保存原始棋谱，只能提供实际保留下来的分析结果。

保存、管理与删除

服务器上的设备、会员、任务和交易记录在提供相关服务、维护购买权益及防止重复处理所需期间保留。主动分享的棋谱可在 App 内撤回；对话和学习记忆可在 App 内清空。崩溃与卡顿诊断保留 180 天后删除。第三方模型供应商的保留期限由其自身政策决定。删除 App 不会自动删除钥匙串、iCloud 或服务器记录；如需查询或删除服务器数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会核实请求并处理。

第三方、跟踪与儿童

我们不出售数据，不使用广告标识符，也不将数据与其他公司的应用或网站数据结合用于广告跟踪。App 不含第三方广告或分析 SDK；本机引擎是开源 KataGo（MIT 许可）。Apple 提供支付与 iCloud 服务，用户选择的模型供应商提供可选 AI 问答。App 不提供定向广告；监护人如需管理或删除未成年人主动提供的数据，可联系我们。

政策变更与联系

我们会在此页更新数据处理说明；采集范围变化时会在 App 内告知并按需要重新征得同意。如有疑问或数据管理请求，请联系：3392236373@qq.com。

Privacy Policy (English)

Updated November 8, 2026. KataGo play, problems, lessons, and local review run on your device without account registration. Membership validation, learning rewards, sharing, optional third-party AI questions, and iCloud synchronization use a network. Cloud play and cloud review, available to paid members, send the relevant game to our servers for computation (see Cloud play and review below). The Mac app does not offer the iOS screen-coaching and camera features.

Data and purposes. We process random device and account identifiers for membership device management and rewards; device model, OS and app version for service operation; Apple-signed transaction identifiers, receipts and app-account tokens for purchase verification; and learning-task progress and reward records for rewards. Records can be linked to the same device or account. Apple does not provide Apple ID passwords or payment-card details through these receipts.

Diagnostics. While a model runs, iOS also collects performance measurements, including memory and CPU use, thermal and battery status, disk I/O and network-interface byte counters, to improve performance and stability. Network counters describe the device as a whole, not browsing history or communication contents. The Mac app does not run this periodic performance sampler. Feedback descriptions, contact details and diagnostic logs that you choose to send are used to respond and troubleshoot.

Crash diagnostics. Unlike the performance data above, each crash report requires your separate consent, so it is described here on its own. It does not change the statement above that play, problems, lessons and local review run on your device: these reports contain no game content, and on-device computation does not move to the cloud because of them. They do not contain games, moves or review content, your name, email or phone number, your AI conversations or learning memories, or any advertising identifier or identifier that could track you across apps or companies; the call stack is generated by the system and describes where our own code failed. To be accurate about one point: the upload uses the same authenticated channel as membership services, so we record the random device identifier that sent it and the server receive time, which, like the other server records described above, can be linked to the same device but is not an advertising identifier. You are asked only once, at the next launch after a crash or a long hang, whether to send that report; it is uploaded only if you tap Send, and tapping Don't Send deletes it on the device, with no second prompt and no later retry. The app never reports crashes automatically, and your choice does not affect any feature or entitlement. The report is produced by Apple's MetricKit framework, already redacted by the system, and forwarded verbatim: per-thread call stacks (function and module names, address offsets), exception type and termination reason, the time the report was produced, device model, OS version, app version and build, physical memory size and the corresponding memory tier, and the AI model tier in use at the time (for example b10 / b28 / b40; empty when unavailable). It is used only to locate and fix crashes and hangs and to confirm that a fix worked — never for advertising, user profiling, sharing with third parties, or sale. There is no third-party crash-reporting SDK; reports go directly to our own server and are deleted after 180 days. You may decline every time; to have a report you already sent deleted, email us the approximate time and device model at the address below and we will verify and delete the matching record.

Game sharing. When you choose to create a sharing link, that SGF, including any player names, comments, moves and result already in it, is uploaded to our server. Anyone with the link can view it. You can revoke shared links in the app.

Your own model API. When you submit a question, relevant questions, conversation history, lesson/problem text, board, SGF, KataGo results and learning memories are sent directly to your configured provider. API keys stay in a non-synchronizing local Keychain item and are not sent to our server or iCloud. The provider may charge and retain content under its own terms and privacy policy. Games, preferences, conversations and learning memories may sync through your Apple iCloud account. Synchronization and conversation/memory deletion controls are available in the app.

Cloud play and review. Cloud play and cloud whole-game review are available to paid members (yearly or lifetime) and are used only when you choose them: when you select the Cloud engine in Custom game, that game's position and moves are sent to our servers, where KataGo computes replies and analysis; when you submit a cloud review, the selected game record (SGF) is uploaded. The server keeps the game, task status, results and credit records needed to complete the computation and settlement, and a cloud game can be resumed after a disconnection. Credits can be bought and used only by paid members and are settled from server records. On-device play, problems and local review do not move to the cloud because of these features.

Cloud review archives. Positions, moves or your selected SGF are uploaded only when you choose a cloud feature. Original review games (including existing player names, comments and variations), task status and reports are retained in your account for asynchronous completion, restart recovery and retrieval across devices. Reading an existing report does not rerun analysis or charge credits again. Games and reports require authentication to the owning account and are not automatically shared publicly. Older tasks without a retained original game can only provide the analysis data that still exists.

Retention and deletion. Device, membership, task and transaction records are retained as needed to provide services, maintain entitlements and prevent duplicate processing. Shared links can be revoked, and conversations and learning memories can be cleared in the app. A model provider sets its own retention period. Uninstalling does not automatically erase Keychain, iCloud or server records. Contact us to request access to or deletion of server data; requests will be verified and handled.

Third parties and tracking. We do not sell data, use advertising identifiers, or combine data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising tracking. There are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Apple provides payments and iCloud; your chosen model provider supplies optional AI responses. KataGo is open source under the MIT license. There is no targeted advertising. Guardians can contact us about data a child has supplied.

We update this policy when practices change and provide in-app notice and renewed consent where needed. Questions or data requests: 3392236373@qq.com.
